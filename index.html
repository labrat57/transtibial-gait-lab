Pylon alignment · tighten or loosen the screws

Press ↻ to tighten a screw (clockwise) or ↺ to loosen it (counter-clockwise). Each screw pushes the pyramid it presses on away from its own side. At the proximal adapter that is the socket; at the distal adapter it is the foot. Tighten one screw and loosen its partner to tilt the adapter.

Source for gait-cycle timing: Chambers HG, Sutherland DH. A Practical Guide to Gait Analysis. Journal of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons. 2002;10:222–231. This is a simplified educational animation, not a patient-specific model.
